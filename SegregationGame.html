🗑️ Waste Sorting Game

How to Play ?

• Waste items fall from the top.
• Read the item name carefully.
• Tap the correct bin to collect it.
• Correct choices increase your score.
• Wrong or missed items grow the garbage mountain.
🏆 Pass by scoring 50 points!
The game ends when garbage mountain reaches the bins.

Best of luck! 💪

How to Segregate Waste ?

🟢

Wet Waste

Food and kitchen waste that can rot or decompose naturally.

Examples: 🍌 Banana Peels, 🍎 Fruit Scraps, 🥕 Vegetable Waste, 🍚 Leftover Food

💡 Tip: Throw compostable bags and garden waste in this bin too.

🔵

Dry Waste

Clean and dry waste that can often be recycled.

Examples: 📄 Paper, 📦 Cardboard, 🧴 Plastic Bottles, 🥫 Metal Cans

💡 Tip: Rinse containers before throwing them in this bin.

🩷

Sanitary Waste

Personal hygiene waste that can spread germs and must be handled carefully.

Examples: 😷 Masks, 🧤 Gloves, 🩲 Diapers, 🧻 Used Tissues

💡 Tip: Wrap sanitary waste in paper before disposing.

🔴

Special Care Waste

Dangerous waste that can harm people or the environment if not disposed properly.

Examples: 🔋 Batteries, 💊 Medicines, 💉 Syringes, 🧲 Magnets

💡 Tip: Never mix this waste with regular household trash.
