# City Sort 3D model credits

Imported models below are CC0 public domain models downloaded from Poly Pizza. The game also creates additional street, bin, and sorting-item geometry locally in Three.js.

- Instructor — “Hoodie Character” by Quaternius, https://poly.pizza/m/gKLBoRsyKe (CC0 1.0)
- Player character — “Adventurer” by Quaternius, https://poly.pizza/m/5EGWBMpuXq (CC0 1.0)
- Buildings — “Business Building” by Quaternius, https://poly.pizza/m/GoF3zph6WI (CC0 1.0)
- Trees — “Tree” by Quaternius, https://poly.pizza/m/s8XdT4FOZz (CC0 1.0)
- Car — “Car” by Quaternius, https://poly.pizza/m/unqqkULtRU (CC0 1.0)
- Sorting-bin base — “Trashcan” by Kenney, https://poly.pizza/m/eoC8NFaafh (CC0 1.0)

The corresponding GLB files are in `models/`. Each linked model page identifies its asset as CC0.

The following CC0 PBR texture sets from Poly Haven add asphalt, pavement, brick, and concrete detail. Their 1K diffuse, normal, and roughness maps are in `textures/`.

- Asphalt 07 — https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_07
- Concrete Pavement — https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_pavement
- Brick Wall 001 — https://polyhaven.com/a/brick_wall_001
- Concrete Wall 001 — https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_001
- Urban Street 01 — photo-based 360° city environment, https://polyhaven.com/a/urban_street_01

The local `vendor/` folder includes the Three.js runtime and GLTFLoader (MIT licenses) and PeerJS client (MIT license); their license texts are included alongside the files.
