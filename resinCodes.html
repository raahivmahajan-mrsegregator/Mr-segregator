Resin Codes Guide

Plastic resin codes are numbers (1–7) found inside the recycling triangle symbol on plastic products.
They tell you exactly what type of plastic it is and how it should be sorted and recycled.

1

PET / PETE

Polyethylene Terephthalate

Water bottles, soft drink bottles, food jars.

Widely Recyclable
2

HDPE

High-Density Polyethylene

Milk jugs, detergent bottles, shampoo containers.

Widely Recyclable
3

PVC

Polyvinyl Chloride

Pipes, cling wrap, some packaging.

Rarely Recyclable
4

LDPE

Low-Density Polyethylene

Grocery bags, bread bags, squeeze bottles.

Increasingly Recyclable
5

PP

Polypropylene

Yogurt containers, bottle caps, food storage.

Commonly Recyclable
6

PS

Polystyrene

Foam cups, takeout containers, packing peanuts.

Difficult to Recycle
7

Other

Mixed / Miscellaneous

Polycarbonate, bioplastics, multi-layer plastics.

Varies by Facility
