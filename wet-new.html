🌱 What is Wet Waste?

Wet waste is biodegradable organic waste that decomposes naturally and can be converted into biogas or rich compost.

✅ Examples of Wet Waste

Kitchen Scraps

Vegetable peels, fruit rinds, leftover food

Tea & Coffee Grounds

Used tea leaves, coffee filters, grounds

Eggshells & Bones

Crushed eggshells, small bones

Garden Waste

Leaves, grass clippings, flowers

Spoiled Food Paper

Paper with food and grease on it

Compostable Bag

100% Compostable Bag

Compostable Packaging

Compostable food packaging

Left Over Food

Bread, rice, veggies, fruits, etc.

❌ What NOT to Put in Wet Waste

Mixing plastic with wet waste makes compost USELESS.

🟢 Bin for Wet Waste

GREEN BIN

  • Lid preferred
  • Washed daily
  • Use compostable liners or newspaper

♻️ How Wet Waste is Processed

  • ✅ Home compost pits
  • ✅ Community composting units
  • ✅ Centralised compost plants
  • ✅ Bio-methanation
