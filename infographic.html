Halloween Waste Segregation Guide

Sort the spooky and sustainable. Keep our haunted world clean and green!

Wet Waste (Biodegradable)

Food scraps, garden waste, and coffee grounds belong in wet waste.

Haunt tip: Compost scraps at home. Keep bones and dairy out of home compost.

Dry Waste (Recyclable)

Paper, cardboard, clean and dry plastics, glass bottles, and metal cans.

Haunt tip: Rinse, dry, and crush recyclables before sorting.

Sanitary Waste (Non-recyclable)

Diapers, pads, tissues, and medical waste. Wrap securely and use the designated bin.

Haunt tip: Seal waste tightly to prevent leaks and odors.

Special Care Waste (Hazardous)

Batteries, e-waste, bulbs, hazardous chemicals, and sharps need careful handling.

Haunt tip: Never mix these items. Take them to a designated drop-off point.
