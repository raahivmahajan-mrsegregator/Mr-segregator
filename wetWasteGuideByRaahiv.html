Wet Waste Segregation Guide

Wet waste, also known as organic or biodegradable waste, includes items that decompose naturally and can be turned into compost. Proper segregation helps in recycling and reduces landfill burden. Always use the green bin for wet waste!

Examples of Wet Waste

Dry Leaves & Garden Waste

Compostable Bags

Tea/Coffee Grounds

Compostable Trays (e.g., Bagasse)

Fruit & Vegetable Scraps

Eggshells

Leftover Food (e.g., Rice)

Soiled Paper Napkins

How to Segregate Wet Waste

  • Collect all organic waste like food scraps, peels, and garden trimmings separately.
  • Rinse lightly if needed to remove excess liquids, but avoid mixing with dry waste.
  • Use compostable bags or liners in your kitchen bin for easy transfer.
  • Dispose in the designated green wet waste bin for collection or composting.
💡 Tip: Start a home compost bin to turn wet waste into nutrient-rich soil for your plants!
